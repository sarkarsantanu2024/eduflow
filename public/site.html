Straight answers

Questions center owners actually ask

Why is EduFlow so affordable?expand_more

Three reasons. It's built for Indian centers, so there's no bloat you'd never use. It's sold directly, so no reseller margin. And WhatsApp reminders go from your own number, so there are no per-message charges to pass on. Those savings go into the price instead of into middlemen.

What happens when I reach 100 students?expand_more

Everything you already have keeps working exactly as it is — no data is touched and no feature switches off. To admit student number 101 you just need room for them: add a seat pack (+25, +50 or +100 students), or move to Growth when that works out cheaper. Tap the pack you want and WhatsApp opens with your centre details already filled in; we reply with payment details and switch the seats on the same day. The app shows your remaining seats well before you run out, so an admission is never held up by surprise.

Can I upgrade anytime?expand_more

Yes — upgrade or downgrade whenever you like and we adjust the difference on your next bill. No upgrade fee, no re-setup, no data migration. Your students, fees and history stay exactly where they are.

Can I bring my existing Excel list?expand_more

Yes. EduFlow has a built-in importer — upload your Excel or CSV file and every student, parent contact and batch is created in one go. It takes a few minutes, you do it yourself whenever you like, and nothing is retyped. If the file needs tidying first, we'll show you exactly what the columns should look like on your demo call.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Any time, from your own account. Export everything to Excel in one click first — every table downloads, so your data leaves with you. No cancellation charge and no lock-in contract.

I'm not technical. Can I really use it?expand_more

If you can use WhatsApp, you can use EduFlow. We set the center up for you, import your students and walk you through it on a call. Most owners are collecting fees through it the same week.

Do I pay extra for WhatsApp messages?expand_more

No — on every plan, including Free. The message opens ready-written on your own WhatsApp with the parent's name, the amount and your UPI QR filled in, and you press send. Unlimited, from your own number, at no cost.

Are the WhatsApp messages sent automatically?expand_more

No, and we want to be straight about that. EduFlow writes the message for you — the parent's name, the amount due and your UPI QR are already filled in — and opens it in your own WhatsApp. You press send. Nothing goes out while your phone is off. That is exactly why the messages are free and unlimited: they come from your number, not from a paid messaging API, so there are no per-message charges and parents see a message from someone they know.

I run several activities in one center. Does that work?expand_more

Yes — choose the multi-activity setup. Every activity (abacus, computer, yoga, dance, drawing, music, karate, spoken English and more) gets its own batches, timings and instructor, with kits, exams, belts, grades, competitions and events switched on — and you still see one fee register and one profit figure for the whole center.
